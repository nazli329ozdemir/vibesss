Cleaning

A little chemistry helps lift, dissolve, and freshen.

NaHCO3Kitchen staple

Baking soda

Also called sodium bicarbonate. It helps neutralize odors and gives baked goods a lift.

Found inFridge · sink · pantry
CH3COOHPantry helper
CH3—COOH

Acetic acid

The tangy acid in vinegar. Diluted vinegar can help dissolve mineral deposits on some surfaces.

Found inVinegar · pickles
H2O2Gentle oxidizer
H—O—O—H

Hydrogen peroxide

A simple compound used in some household products for its oxidizing action.

Found inSome cleaners · first aid
