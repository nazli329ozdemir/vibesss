THESE CAN HELP YOU

Everyday Facts

💧

Water is a great mixer

Water can dissolve many substances, which is one reason it’s useful in kitchens and in our bodies.

🫧

Baking soda makes bubbles

When baking soda meets an acid, they can react and release carbon dioxide gas—the bubbles that help some batters rise.

🍋

Lemons have citric acid

That naturally occurring acid helps give lemons and other citrus fruits their tart taste.

🧂

Salt separates in water

When table salt dissolves, it separates into tiny charged particles called sodium and chloride ions.

🥤

Fizz comes from a gas

The bubbles in fizzy drinks are carbon dioxide gas escaping from the liquid.

🧼

Soap helps lift oily dirt

Soap molecules can interact with both water and oils, helping water wash oily dirt away.

A little care goes a long way

Everyday chemistry, used safely.

A few simple habits can help make everyday products safer to use.

01

Read the label

Follow the product’s directions and warnings each time you use it.

📖
02

Don’t mix cleaners

Mixing household cleaning products can create dangerous fumes.

⚠️
03

Let fresh air in

Open a window or use a fan when the product label recommends ventilation.

🌬️
04

Store products safely

Keep products in their original labeled containers, away from children and pets.
